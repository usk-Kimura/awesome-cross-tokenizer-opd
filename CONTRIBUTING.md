# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep this survey accurate. Corrections are as valuable as
new-paper suggestions.

## Before proposing a core paper

Please verify all of the following in the public full text:

- the training trajectory or visited state is generated by the student/current
  policy;
- teacher and student use explicitly different tokenizers or vocabularies;
- the paper describes how a teacher signal crosses that mismatch; and
- at least one mismatched teacher–student pair is actually trained and reported.

Cross-family model names, a sentence about tokenizer mismatch in the
introduction, or separate claims that a framework supports OPD and
cross-tokenizer KD are not sufficient on their own.

## What to include

For an addition or correction, provide:

1. the primary paper/venue URL and exact public version checked;
2. section, equation, table, appendix, or public-code permalink for each claim;
3. teacher and student names in **Teacher → Student** order;
4. trajectory source, comparison interface, feedback coverage, teacher access,
   and probability/mass semantics;
5. official code/project URL and its status; and
6. any ambiguity, missing implementation detail, or mismatch between paper and
   code.

Do not copy abstracts or long passages. Write an original, compact summary and
link to the source.

## Pull requests

- Update the English `README.md`; update `README_ja.md` when the taxonomy or a
  central conclusion changes.
- Add or revise the corresponding record in `data/papers.yml`.
- Add a BibTeX entry to `references.bib` for a new paper.
- Preserve the distinction among core methods, evidence-caveat rows, boundary
  applications, frameworks/resources, and off-policy enabling work.
- Set `last_verified` only to the date on which links and claims were checked.

Use the issue forms if you would rather submit evidence without editing the
repository.

## Evidence and tone

“Verified” means checked against public primary material, not experimentally
reproduced. Attribute numerical or exactness claims to the paper, state negative
findings narrowly (for example, “no official code found as of DATE”), and make
uncertainty visible instead of filling gaps by inference.

By contributing, you agree that your changes to this survey's metadata and
original summaries are released under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE). Linked papers, code,
and third-party materials retain their own licenses.
